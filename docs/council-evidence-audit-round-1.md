# Evidence Audit — Round 1 (sealed)

## A. Empiricist
- Only the move-in report's 14-day deadline has an official source.
- The home page's item priority rules, 48 checklist items, claim that links are not affiliate/advertising, and local-storage wording remain unverified or overbroad.
- FAQ structured data may not correspond to visible page content.

## B. Systems Thinker
- The main long-term risk is repeated exposure of unvalidated recommendations through SEO and persistent local data.
- Budget math is direct arithmetic, but omitted expense categories can make first-month totals look complete.
- `localStorage` lacks clear retention, deletion, and browser/profile limitation notice.

## C. Adversary
- Stop expanding the current personalized-rule system.
- Three housing options and three lifestyle options cannot justify fixed `must/later/skip` recommendations.
- Checklist progress can imply readiness despite missing contract and deposit-related concerns.

## D. Pragmatist
- Do not build new features before one-day validation with five recent movers.
- Ask for three needed, three unnecessary, and three missing items; record whether the generated grouping was useful.
- Retain arithmetic only; judge links by whether they helped the next action, not clicks.

## E. Steward
- A first renter may mistake the interface for complete contractual guidance.
- The 48-item progress indicator can look like a validated standard.
- Zero defaults in the budget can understate unknown costs.
